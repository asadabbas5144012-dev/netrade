How Referral Rewards Work
Your referral balance starts at zero, and sharing your referral link alone does not earn you anything.

When someone signs up using your referral link, they become part of your network — but if that person deposits before you have made your own first deposit, you will not receive any reward from it. Referral rewards are only paid to accounts that have themselves made at least one confirmed deposit.

Once you make your own first deposit, you become eligible to earn. From that point on, whenever anyone in your referral network (the people you referred directly, and the people they referred, up to 5 levels deep) makes a qualifying deposit, you will automatically earn a percentage of that deposit based on your level in the chain and the platform's current reward rates — credited straight to your balance.

In short: build your team any time, but deposit yourself first if you want to actually start earning from their deposits.
